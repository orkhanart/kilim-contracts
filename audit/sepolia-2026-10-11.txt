Sepolia rehearsal, deployed 11 October 2026 (chain 11155111, Glamsterdam)
==========================================================================

The full launch on the Sepolia testnet, the two phases as on mainnet (checklists/0-launch-steps.md steps 5
and 6, checklists/2-deploy.md), from the final artwork (mint/artwork.html SHA-256 55f33486…, WebP still).
First store transaction at block 11,893,078 (17:01:36 UTC), auction at block 11,893,419 (18:09:48 UTC).
Sepolia's block gas limit was about 200M. Nothing was sent on Ethereum mainnet: orkhanart.eth's mainnet
nonce stayed 1,568 throughout (a watch stopped forge if it moved).

forge 1.7.1, solc 0.8.37, the contracts and script at commit 3548b8cb (no source change since).
RPC for sending: https://sepolia.drpc.org. Gas flags on every command: --skip-simulation
--gas-estimate-multiplier 125 (store) or 120 (contracts, display), --with-gas-price 20000000
--priority-gas-price 2000000. The base fee was a few wei, so every transaction paid about 0.002 gwei.

What the rehearsal taught (for mainnet)
---------------------------------------
- forge's browser wallet serves its page at http://127.0.0.1:9545 and the page's code posts to exactly
  that origin: localhost:9545 or another port fails. Use Chrome with MetaMask, pick MetaMask in the page's
  wallet list (another installed wallet answered first once, on its own chain), and make MetaMask show the
  right network before Connect. The page waits 5 minutes for a connection, then forge gives up. Between
  transactions the page can sit on "No pending transaction"; reloading it brings the next request, and a
  request that MetaMask already holds is still sent if confirmed after forge stops: reject stale
  requests in MetaMask before a rerun (the rerun skips whatever landed).
- For 45 chunks the browser wallet is too slow by hand: send the store from a separate wallet with a key
  forge can use (the checklist's store wallet), and keep the browser wallet for the three contracts.
- forge reads a keystore only with a lowercase "crypto" field; ethers v6 writes "Crypto".
- DisplayTest.s.sol holds two contracts: its command needs --tc DisplayTest (fixed in the checklist).
- Public RPCs answered every 24.6 KB eth_estimateGas in well under a second.
